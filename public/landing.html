FAQ

Questions.

Do I need a YouTube API key?

No. The YouTube Data API only exposes metadata and can't download anything. Grab uses yt-dlp directly.

Why does 4K say "AV1 · modern player"?

YouTube has no H.264 above 1080p, so 1440p and 4K are AV1 or VP9. They play in Chrome, Edge, VLC, Windows 11 Media Player and modern phones/TVs.

Can I get just the audio?

Yes — MP3 (re-encoded at the best VBR quality with cover art) or M4A (YouTube's original AAC stream, untouched).

Is this allowed?

Downloading is against YouTube's Terms of Service unless the creator permits it. Grab is for personal use — your own content, Creative Commons, lectures, offline listening. Respect creators' rights.
